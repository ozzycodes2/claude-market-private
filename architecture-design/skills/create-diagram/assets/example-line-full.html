FOCAL · ORGANIC

Organic search was 55% of W8 signups

Organic grew +75% over 8 weeks, from 120 to 210 signups a week. Two long-form guides published in W4 lifted it from 132 to 170 in one week, against +6 a week before. The W5 dip, from 170 to 162, is a public holiday.

Direct: stable core

+22.5% over 8 weeks (80→98). The near-flat slope is a healthy baseline, not stagnation: the direct audience holds while organic expands.

Referral: early signal

+67% over 8 weeks (45→75). Volatile week to week: it dipped in W3, W5 and W7 and recovered each time. Worth a dedicated channel review before investing further in partner programmes.
