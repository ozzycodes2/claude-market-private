Latin America: a step in W4

Latin America went from 23 to 41 signups a week in W4, the week the Portuguese site launched, then kept climbing: from 22 to 67 over 8 weeks, about three times its W1 level.

Asia Pacific: a steady climb

Asia Pacific rose from 48 to 85 with no step: its largest weekly gain is 7, so the slope is the story, not one event.

What the chart doesn't show

One shared scale makes Africa (12 to 24) look small beside North America (96 to 108), which is the point of the scale but hides that Africa doubled (+100%). Compare shapes across panels, growth rates within one.
