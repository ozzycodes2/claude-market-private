Pyramid · Content pyramid, what compounds

Each layer ships less often than the one below

Four layers of output, ordered by how rarely they ship and how far they travel. The base keeps you present. The apex defines the body of work — and it's the only layer anyone quotes back a decade later.

THE HEADLINE

One accent layer, on purpose

A five-colour pyramid is a children's diagram. Reserving the accent for the apex makes the whole structure actually say something: this is the rarest thing you'll make, and it's the one the rest of the pyramid is feeding.

Width is rank, not quantity

Each layer steps in by the same amount. The per-year counts (~240, ~48, ~4, one every 3–5 years) are printed labels; trapezoid area does not encode them.

Pyramids only work for hierarchy

If your four categories don't have a rarity order — if a bullet list would communicate it — use bullets. Here the order is cadence, from Short posts at the base to the Flagship book at the apex; a pyramid promises you're trading volume for value as you climb.
