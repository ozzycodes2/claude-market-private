Treemap · World population, six continents

Asia holds 59% of world population

Population by continent, mid-2023. Asia holds 4.78 billion people — 59% of everyone alive, and more than the other five continents put together.

One cell carries the story

Asia is the focal cell because it is the finding, not because it sorts first. Nearly six of every ten people alive are inside that rectangle.

How to read it

Cells are laid out squarified, largest first (Asia, then Africa), so aspect ratios stay near 1 and areas stay comparable by eye. Among the other five, stronger contrast means larger.

What the chart doesn't show

Population, not growth. Africa's cell is under a third of Asia's today, and it is the one adding people fastest — this is one moment, not a direction.
