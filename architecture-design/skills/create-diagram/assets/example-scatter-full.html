Scatter · Deploy frequency vs. lead time, 12 teams

Teams that deploy more often ship faster

Each point is an engineering team. Moving right means deploying more often; moving down means shipping faster. The Platform team occupies the lower-right — the ideal quadrant.

NAMED TEAM

Platform team · (18, 3)

Platform deploys 18 times a week with a 3-day lead time. Only the team at (20, 2) is ahead of it on both measures, so Platform sits in the lower-right corner with it.

Correlation is clear

The least squares fit slopes down across all 12 teams, and every team deploying more than nine times a week (the median) has a lead time at or below the 10-day median. Deploy rate and lead time move together; the chart does not show that either causes the other.

Two outliers worth examining

The team at (2, 20) ships rarely and slowly; possible approval chains are marked on the chart as a hypothesis. The team at (12, 10) deploys often but sits on the median lead time; possible slow integration tests are marked, also unconfirmed.
